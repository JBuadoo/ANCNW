House of Grace

Welcome to All Nations Church NorthWest

A place to come discover.

At All Nations Church NorthWest, we believe every person is precious to God. We are a multicultural congregation united by the love of Jesus Christ.

Whether you are taking your first steps in faith, seeking renewal, or looking for a church family in the greater Seattle area, our doors and hearts are open to you.

  • Grace-centered preaching
  • Multicultural worship
  • Kids & youth programs
  • Community life groups

      Our Church

      About Us

      A multicultural body of Christ committed to the Word of God and to loving all people.

      Our Mission

      Restoring people and releasing their potential

      By connecting them to God through sound biblical teaching, community, and the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

      Our Vision

      Expose, Enable, Equip, and Enrich

      We aim to expose people to the truth of God's Word, enable them to grow, equip them for service, and enrich their lives and communities.

      What We Stand On

      Core values

      1. 01

        Grace

        Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, both justifying and empowering.

      2. 02

        The Word

        The Bible is God's inspired truth and our highest authority for life and practice.

      3. 03

        All Nations

        We celebrate every tribe, ethnicity, and generation as one family in Christ.

      4. 04

        Prayer

        We seek God's presence, power, and breakthrough through praying together.
