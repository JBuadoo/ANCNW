Welcome

A Place to Belong

At All Nations Church NorthWest, we believe every person is precious to God. We are a multicultural congregation united by the love of Jesus Christ.

Whether you are taking your first steps in faith, seeking renewal, or looking for a church family in the greater Seattle area — our doors and hearts are open to you.

Our Church

About Us

A multicultural body of Christ committed to the Word of God and loving all people.

Our Mission

Restoring People and Releasing Their Potential

By connecting them to God through sound biblical teaching, community, and the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

Expose, Enable, Equip, and Enrich

We aim to expose people to the truth of God's Word, enable them to grow, equip them for service, and enrich their lives and communities.
